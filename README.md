<a id="overview"></a>

**Spelunky** is an addictive platform game that invites players to become a daring speleologist on a quest to recover hidden treasures and rescue a loved one trapped within perilous tunnels. Available on **Windows**, this game combines classic 8-bit graphics with engaging gameplay mechanics that keep players returning for more. The **latest version** of Spelunky is packed with enhancements and improvements, ensuring a thrilling experience for both new and returning players.

Dive into a world where you must navigate through treacherous caves, collect treasures, and defeat enemies using a variety of tools including a whip, climbing ropes, and explosives. With its comprehensive feature set, Spelunky offers an immersive adventure that is both fun and rewarding.
